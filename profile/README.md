No résumés. No gatekeepers. No credentials checked at the door.

The dropout beats the consultant if their solution is better. The kid with a laptop beats the agency if their answer is sharper. **The credential doesn't win. The solution does.**

---

## How it works

A sponsor posts a real problem with a prize — cash in escrow, public credit, or a named cause. Solvers compete. The best solution wins. Anonymous on both sides if you want it that way.

Every win goes on a public solver profile: permanent, portable, exportable as a PDF credential. Not what you claim to be. What you actually did.

> *Drop out. Go west. Go find one.*

---

## The prize model

| Prize | What the winner gets |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | Money. Any amount. Released from escrow when you pick a winner. |
| **Credit** | A public win on their solver profile — permanent and exportable. |
| **Cause** | Credit plus a named cause attached to the win. |

No floor. No ceiling. A charity with a $0 budget competes on the same board as a funded startup.
