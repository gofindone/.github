Real problems. Real prize money in escrow. Anyone can compete — no résumé, no credential, no gatekeeper.

The dropout beats the consultant if their solution is better. The kid with a laptop beats the agency if their answer is sharper. **The credential doesn't win. The solution does.**

---

## How it works

A sponsor posts a real problem with prize money locked in escrow. Solvers compete. The best solution wins. The prize pays out. No winner? Full refund.
