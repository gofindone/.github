## What is GoFindOne?

A sponsor posts a real problem with real prize money locked in escrow. Solvers compete. The best solution wins. The prize pays out.

That's it. No profiles. No bidding wars. No résumé review.

The dropout beats the consultant if their solution is better. The kid with a laptop beats the agency if their answer is sharper. **The credential doesn't win. The solution does.**

---

## The flip

Every freelance platform works the same way: you scroll profiles, pick someone, hope for the best.

GoFindOne inverts it. The sponsor posts the problem. The solvers come to them. Open competition. Blind evaluation. Work that speaks for itself.

> *Drop out. Go west. Go find one.*

---

## How it works

| For sponsors | For solvers |
|---|---|
| Post your problem | Browse open challenges |
| Set the prize | Submit your solution |
| Prize goes into escrow | Best solution wins |
| Pick the winner | Prize pays out |
| No winner? Full refund | No entry fee |
