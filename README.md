# 1. GOFIND.ONE

**One problem. One prize. One winner.**

GoFindOne is a prize-based challenge platform where sponsors post real problems with real money in escrow — and anyone, regardless of credentials, can compete to solve them.

No résumés. No gatekeepers. The work decides.

---

## How it works

A sponsor defines a problem, sets a prize, and puts the money in escrow. Solvers compete. The best solution wins. The prize pays out. If nothing qualifies, the sponsor gets a full refund.

The credential doesn't win. The solution does.

---

## The flip

Most platforms make buyers scroll through seller profiles. GoFindOne inverts that. The sponsor posts the problem. The solvers come to them. It's not about who looks best on paper — it's about who solves it best in practice.
